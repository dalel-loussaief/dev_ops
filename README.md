# 📦 Purchase Management System

## 🚀 Overview

The **Purchase Management System** is a professional web application designed to streamline and optimize the procurement process within an organization. It allows managing suppliers, purchase orders, product catalogs, stock reception, and invoice tracking — all from a centralized dashboard.

---

## 🎯 Key Features

- ✅ **Supplier Management** – Add, edit, delete, and categorize suppliers.
- ✅ **Product Management** – Maintain product catalog with prices, references, and stock alerts.
- ✅ **Purchase Orders** – Create, validate, and track purchase orders.
- ✅ **Order Reception** – Record incoming products and partial deliveries.
- ✅ **Invoice Management** – Link invoices to orders and monitor payments.
- ✅ **Reporting Dashboard** – Visualize expenses, order statuses, and supplier performance.
- ✅ **User Roles** – Admin, Purchaser, Warehouse Manager, Viewer.
- ✅ **Audit Logs** – Track all changes for compliance.
